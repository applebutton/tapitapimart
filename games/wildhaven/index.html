How to play +

1. Explore. Swipe or drag the world to look around. Use the arrow buttons or WASD to walk. Q / R turn; Page Up / Down look up and down. Watch out for purple wisps!

2. Vacuum. Look toward something and get close. Press Vacuum, Space, or E to scoop up a creature, berries, wood, metal, or moonstones. Big creatures fit too! Carry up to 30 of each kind.

3. Make a home. At the ranch, open Inventory, choose a pen, and put your friends inside. They stay in their pen until you vacuum them out.

4. Build & mix. Use wood and moonstones to build your home. Combine two small friends from your bag into one new giant!

5. Visit the Creator’s Shed. Beside your home, spend earned stardust on clothing, tool skins, pets, furniture, themes, food, and supplies. No real money is used.

Feed and pet penned friends for happiness and stardust gifts. Three wisp bumps send you safely home. Everything you collected stays yours.
