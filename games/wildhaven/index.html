Field notes +

Move with WASD / arrow keys, hold the arrow buttons, or tap the map. Get close to a creature and press Space / E or Catch / gather. Every catch earns 3 stardust.

Pink berry bushes give free treats. At your ranch, feed and pet companions to raise happiness. At 75 happiness they make gifts worth 8 stardust, once a minute.

Glowing purple wisps can bump you. After three hits, you respawn safely at the ranch with everything you collected. There is no ending, timer, or losing.

Your progress saves on this device. Creature happiness gently settles while you are away, but never below 25. Your friends stay forever.
