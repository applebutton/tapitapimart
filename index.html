How to play +

Take turns placing X and O on the board. Get three of your marks in a row, column, or diagonal to win. If the board fills up, it's a draw. The starting player alternates each round. Against the computer, you play X. Easy makes random moves, Medium wins or blocks immediate threats, and Hard plays perfectly. Changing opponent or difficulty starts a new session.
